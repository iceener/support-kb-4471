The fix queued for #4471 fixes only the parser problem. After it ships, the hint and one-day reports still come in.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so the day and the month flip: 7 April becomes 4 July. These reports come in through the app, by email, and through the booking-v2 form.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not change this.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not by months: 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. A month-first parser cannot do that. The reports point at time zones: a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto got different days for the same meeting. The desk marked these reports in its notes. The #4471 table still counts them under parser, because it sorts by tenant only.

How the reports were counted

  1. One report per id. The export’s pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  2. The cause comes from the tenant: its locale and its booking form (causes.ts). The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it is often wrong.
  3. Reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) are a problem of their own. Their texts are below.

What each fix needs

  • parser: read each date in the order of the tenant’s locale. This is the fix queued for #4471.
  • hint: make booking-v3 print the hint in the tenant’s order, or read the typed date in the order the hint shows.
  • one-day: find where a date crosses a time zone (invites, reminders, calendar sync) and keep the date in the zone of the booking.
